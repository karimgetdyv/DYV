Privacy Policy for DYV AI

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

DYV AI ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Baby Cry Translator & White Noise mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle user data, permissions, and third-party services in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

  • Microphone & Audio Data: Our application requires microphone access strictly to record short audio snippets of baby cries to analyze and translate cry patterns. Audio recordings are processed solely for app functionality and are not sold or transferred to unauthorized third parties.
  • Device Identifiers & Diagnostics: We may collect non-personally identifiable device information, crash logs, and performance metrics to ensure app stability and improve user experience.
  • In-App Purchases & Subscriptions: Financial transactions for premium subscriptions are processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not store or handle your credit card or bank details on our servers.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

DYV AI uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for analytics and personalized advertising:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob: Serves banner and interstitial advertisements inside the app. AdMob may use device advertising IDs to deliver relevant ads. You can manage your ad preferences via your Android device settings.

3. Data Protection & Retention

All data transmitted between the app and external services utilizes secure, encrypted communication protocols (HTTPS). We retain user data only for as long as necessary to provide the features described in the app.

4. Children's Privacy

DYV AI is designed as a utility tool for parents, guardians, and adult caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

5. User Data Rights & Deletion

You have the right to request access to or deletion of any personal data associated with your usage of DYV AI. To submit a data deletion request or inquire about your privacy settings, please contact us at support@getdyv.com.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published directly to this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
